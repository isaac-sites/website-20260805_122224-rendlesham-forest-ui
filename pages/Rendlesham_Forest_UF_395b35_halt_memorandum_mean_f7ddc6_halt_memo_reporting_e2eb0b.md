## Introduction

Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s memorandum was sent to the British Ministry of Defence (MoD) for administrative and jurisdictional reasons, not because the United States Air Force had concluded that an extraordinary event had occurred. The reported sightings took place outside the perimeter of RAF [Woodbridge]({{ 'woodbridge/' | relative_url }}) on British territory, even though the base itself was operated by the US Air Force. As a result, the established reporting route was through the Royal Air Force (RAF) liaison system to the MoD, the department responsible for UK air defence and matters affecting British territory. This reporting path explains why the memorandum entered official British government files, but it does not mean that the MoD endorsed the witnesses' interpretation or regarded the incident as evidence of an unidentified craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/rendlesham-forest-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[Dr. David Clarke]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dr. David Clarke Rendlesham Forest UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dr. David ClarkeRendlesham Forest UFOs &#124;December 31, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2020</span></span></span>

## The RAF liaison request that prompted the memorandum

The immediate reason for the memorandum was a request from the RAF liaison officer attached to the [Bentwaters]({{ 'bentwaters/' | relative_url }})–Woodbridge complex. According to later accounts from Colonel Gordon E. Williams Moreland, who served as the RAF liaison officer, Halt informally described the unusual events after the Christmas 1980 sightings. Moreland asked him to produce a written account so that it could be forwarded through official channels to the MoD. He subsequently attached a covering letter and sent Halt's memorandum to the Air Staff Secretariat (DS8), the MoD office then responsible for handling reports of unidentified aerial phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/rendlesham-forest-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[Dr. David Clarke]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dr. David Clarke Rendlesham Forest UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dr. David ClarkeRendlesham Forest UFOs &#124;December 31, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2020</span></span></span>

This sequence is significant because it shows that the memorandum originated as an administrative response to a liaison request rather than as part of a dedicated American UFO investigation. By late 1980, the US Air Force no longer operated Project Blue Book or any equivalent official programme for investigating UFO reports. Without such a mechanism, the practical route was to notify the British authorities responsible for the surrounding territory.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/rendlesham-forest-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[Dr. David Clarke]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dr. David Clarke Rendlesham Forest UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dr. David ClarkeRendlesham Forest UFOs &#124;December 31, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2020</span></span></span>

The memorandum therefore functioned primarily as an official record of what US personnel reported seeing, allowing the host nation's defence authorities to decide whether any further action was required.

## Why British territory determined the reporting route

Although RAF Woodbridge and RAF Bentwaters were used by the United States Air Force, they remained military installations on British soil under long-standing UK-US defence arrangements. The reported lights were said to be beyond the Woodbridge perimeter, in Rendlesham Forest rather than inside the secured base.

That distinction mattered administratively. If unusual activity appeared outside the installation, questions potentially arose about:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* activity occurring on British territory;
* possible implications for UK air defence;
* whether any civilian or military aircraft had been involved; and
* whether British authorities needed to investigate.

</div>

Because those responsibilities rested with the United Kingdom rather than the United States, the RAF liaison office served as the formal bridge between the American command and the MoD. The memorandum was therefore routed to the British government because of jurisdiction and defence responsibility, not because it represented an agreed Anglo-American assessment of the incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/2020/12/26/rendlesham-ufos-40th-anniversary-the-man-from-the-ministry-speaks-for-the-first-time/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[Dr. David Clarke]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on drdavidclarke.co.uk.</span></span></span>

This also explains why the memorandum bears the neutral subject heading **"[Unexplained Lights]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }})"** rather than language asserting the presence of an extraterrestrial craft. It was framed as an observational report requiring official consideration.

## What the handoff did—and did not—signify

Forwarding the memorandum to the MoD has often been interpreted as evidence that British defence officials regarded the incident as exceptional. The surviving official record supports a narrower conclusion.

Once received, the report was circulated to MoD staff responsible for air defence. Contemporary accounts from former officials indicate that they checked available [radar records]({{ 'radar/' | relative_url }}) and consulted the appropriate operational branches to determine whether the incident suggested any threat to UK airspace. No supporting radar evidence indicating an unidentified intruder was identified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/2020/12/26/rendlesham-ufos-40th-anniversary-the-man-from-the-ministry-speaks-for-the-first-time/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[Dr. David Clarke]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on drdavidclarke.co.uk.</span></span></span>

Subsequent parliamentary answers consistently state that the MoD assessed Halt's memorandum as containing **nothing of [defence significance]({{ 'defence-significance/' | relative_url }})** and therefore took no further investigative action. The department did not issue a formal reply to Halt, nor did it open a major inquiry based solely on the memorandum.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://hansard.parliament.uk/lords/1997-10-14/debates/96394ce5-f0db-4448-af50-43a69fbf3c57/LieutenantColonelCharlesHaltMemorandum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hansard.parliament.uk">[Hansard]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hansard.parliament.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hansard Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt: Memorandum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt: Memorandum - Hansard - UK Parliament...</span></span></span>

The reporting chain therefore achieved its intended administrative purpose:

* it placed the observations before the competent [British authority]({{ 'british-authority/' | relative_url }});

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* it enabled routine defence checks, including consideration of air-defence information;
* it preserved the account within official government records; and
* it allowed the MoD to determine whether the incident required further defence action.

</div>

Those steps demonstrate that the memorandum was treated as a report to be assessed, not as a document establishing that an unidentified craft had been confirmed.

## Why the reporting route still matters

Understanding why Halt sent the memorandum to Britain helps explain one of the most frequently misunderstood aspects of the Rendlesham Forest incident. The memorandum entered British government [archives]({{ 'archives/' | relative_url }}) because the alleged events occurred on British territory and because the RAF liaison system existed precisely to pass such matters from US forces to the host nation's defence authorities.

Its later prominence stems from the fact that it was an official military document signed by a senior US officer. That administrative status gave it lasting historical importance, but it should not be confused with an investigative finding or an official conclusion about what caused the reported lights. The reporting route reflects military procedure and jurisdiction, whereas the MoD's later assessment reflects its separate judgement that the information did not disclose a matter of defence significance.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/2020/12/26/rendlesham-ufos-40th-anniversary-the-man-from-the-ministry-speaks-for-the-first-time/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[drdavidclarke.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on drdavidclarke.co.uk.</span></span></span>
