## Introduction

During the [first night]({{ 'first-night/' | relative_url }}) of the Rendlesham Forest incident, one of the few events documented by an independent civilian authority was a telephone call to Suffolk Constabulary at **4.11am on 26 December 1980**. The record is important because it confirms that RAF Woodbridge personnel considered the sightings sufficiently unusual to involve the local police while the search was still in progress. At the same time, the [police response]({{ 'police-response/' | relative_url }}) produced a notably negative result: officers found no crashed aircraft, no landed object, no wreckage and no physical evidence supporting the presence of an unidentified craft. That combination—a genuine contemporary report followed by an unsuccessful search—makes the police record one of the most significant pieces of documentary evidence from the first night, regardless of how the wider incident is interpreted.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.therendleshamforestincident.com/2022/04/suffolk-constabulary-have-record-dated.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: therendleshamforestincident.com">[The Rendlesham Forest Incident]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">therendleshamforestincident.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">at present</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Rendlesham Forest IncidentSuffolk Constabulary have a record, dated 26 December 1980, of a report from the law Enforcement Desk of RA...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 1980</span></span></span>

## The 4.11am call from RAF Woodbridge

The surviving police log records a call from the RAF [Woodbridge]({{ 'woodbridge/' | relative_url }}) law enforcement desk shortly after four o'clock in the morning. According to the entry, the caller reported:

> "We have a sighting of some unusual lights in the sky... we are terming it as a U.F.O. at present."

The wording is notable for several reasons. First, it refers specifically to **"unusual lights in the sky"**, not to a confirmed landed craft or recovered object. Second, it records that **unarmed troops had already been sent to investigate**, showing that the military search was underway before civilian police became involved. Third, the phrase "terming it as a U.F.O." reflects a working description rather than an official conclusion. In aviation and policing practice, "UFO" simply denoted an unidentified observation until further information became available.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.therendleshamforestincident.com/2022/04/suffolk-constabulary-have-record-dated.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: therendleshamforestincident.com">[The Rendlesham Forest Incident]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">therendleshamforestincident.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">at present</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Rendlesham Forest IncidentSuffolk Constabulary have a record, dated 26 December 1980, of a report from the law Enforcement Desk of RA...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 1980</span></span></span>

The timing also helps establish the chronology of the first night's events. Researchers have long used this police record to demonstrate that the initial incident occurred during the early hours of **26 December 1980**, correcting the one-day dating error that later appeared in Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s memorandum.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-rendlesham-forest-britains-roswell/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[skeptic.org.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: Rendlesham Forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: Rendlesham Forest - Britain’s Roswell? - The Skeptic...</span></span></span>

## What officers searched and recorded

Following the report, Suffolk Constabulary carried out a straightforward police response rather than a specialised investigation into [unexplained]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }}) phenomena.

The surviving record shows that officers:

* checked with **Air Traffic Control at West Drayton**, which had no knowledge of any aircraft accident associated with the report;
* noted reports of aerial phenomena elsewhere over southern England during the night;
* identified the **Orfordness [lighthouse]({{ 'lighthouse/' | relative_url }})** as the only persistent local light visible from the area; and
* conducted a search that was officially recorded simply as **"negative."**<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.therendleshamforestincident.com/2022/04/suffolk-constabulary-have-record-dated.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: therendleshamforestincident.com">[The Rendlesham Forest Incident]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">therendleshamforestincident.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">at present</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Rendlesham Forest IncidentSuffolk Constabulary have a record, dated 26 December 1980, of a report from the law Enforcement Desk of RA...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 1980</span></span></span>

That final word is especially significant. In police terminology, a negative search indicates that the officers did not locate evidence confirming the reported event. The log does not describe debris, impact damage, a landed vehicle, fire, injured persons or any other findings expected after an aircraft accident or confirmed landing.

Subsequent correspondence released from the Suffolk Constabulary files reinforces the same conclusion. One attending constable reportedly returned to the location in daylight in case anything had been overlooked during the night. According to Inspector Mike Topliss's later summary, nothing was found, and the officer remained unconvinced that any extraordinary occurrence had taken place.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.therendleshamforestincident.com/2022/04/suffolk-constabulary-have-record-dated.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: therendleshamforestincident.com">[The Rendlesham Forest Incident]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">therendleshamforestincident.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">at present</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Rendlesham Forest IncidentSuffolk Constabulary have a record, dated 26 December 1980, of a report from the law Enforcement Desk of RA...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 1980</span></span></span>

## Why the lighthouse appears in the police record

The police log's reference to the **Orfordness lighthouse** has become central to later discussions because it represents a contemporary observation rather than a retrospective theory.

The lighthouse, located several miles east of Rendlesham Forest, emitted a regular rotating beam visible from parts of the search area. Investigators including astronomer Ian Ridpath have argued that at least some of the flashing lights reported during the first night align closely with the lighthouse's position and timing. The police officers themselves did not claim this explained every reported observation, but they considered it the only identifiable light source present during their search.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham1a.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original articleJanuary 5, 1985...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 5, 1985</span></span></span>

## Why the negative result still matters

The Suffolk police documents neither prove nor disprove every claim made about the Rendlesham incident. Their importance lies elsewhere.

They establish several points with contemporary documentary support:

* **The report was real.** Military personnel genuinely contacted civilian police while the incident was unfolding.
* **The concern was operational.** The initial response resembled the investigation of a possible aircraft incident rather than an immediate assumption of extraterrestrial activity.
* **The police found no corroborating physical evidence.** Their search produced no landed object, wreckage or other confirming material.
* **Alternative explanations were already being considered.** The officers documented identifiable light sources instead of treating the report as inherently inexplicable.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.therendleshamforestincident.com/2022/04/suffolk-constabulary-have-record-dated.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: therendleshamforestincident.com">[therendleshamforestincident.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">therendleshamforestincident.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">at present</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Rendlesham Forest IncidentSuffolk Constabulary have a record, dated 26 December 1980, of a report from the law Enforcement Desk of RA...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 1980</span></span></span>
