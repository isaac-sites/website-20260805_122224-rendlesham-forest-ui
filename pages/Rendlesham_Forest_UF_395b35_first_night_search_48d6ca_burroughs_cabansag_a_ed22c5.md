## Introduction

John Burroughs and Edward Cabansag took part in the same search through Rendlesham Forest during the first night of the Rendlesham Forest incident, yet their surviving statements do not describe the apparent source of the lights in exactly the same way. Burroughs recalled confusing [moving lights]({{ 'moving-lights/' | relative_url }}), changing points of reference and sounds within the forest before concluding that the principal flashing light was a lighthouse. Cabansag's account is more consistently framed as a pursuit of what gradually proved to be a distant beacon rather than a nearby object. These differences have become important because they illustrate how two observers sharing the same event could interpret changing visual cues differently without necessarily disagreeing about every underlying fact. Contemporary [witness statements]({{ 'witness-dates/' | relative_url }}) therefore provide a more nuanced picture than later simplified retellings of the incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## Burroughs's account of lights and movement

[Burroughs]({{ 'burroughs/' | relative_url }})'s original statement presents an investigation marked by uncertainty rather than a clear encounter with a landed craft. He described seeing coloured lights, hearing unusual noises in the forest and repeatedly changing direction as the apparent source seemed to shift position. His sketch of the lights also differs noticeably from James Penniston's later depiction of a structured triangular object, suggesting that the two men were not recording the same visual experience even though they were participating in the same search.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

One of the most revealing parts of Burroughs's statement comes near the end of the pursuit. After crossing into more open ground near a farmhouse, he wrote that the party could see "a beacon going around" and followed it until they recognised it as a [lighthouse]({{ 'lighthouse/' | relative_url }}). Rather than presenting this as an incidental observation, Burroughs described it as the culmination of the chase, indicating that what had initially appeared to lie within the forest eventually proved to be much farther away.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-the-truth-about-rendlesham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: The Truth about Rendlesham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: The Truth about Rendlesham - The Skeptic...</span></span></span>

Burroughs nevertheless did not portray the entire experience as simply misidentifying a lighthouse from the outset. His statement includes references to blue lights among the trees, strange movement and unsettling sounds that contributed to the confusion. Researchers such as Ian Ridpath have suggested that some of these nearer lights may have originated from responding police vehicles, while the reported screams are consistent with the calls of muntjac deer, an explanation strengthened by similar sounds heard by Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }}) two nights later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## Cabansag's distant beacon interpretation

[Cabansag]({{ 'cabansag/' | relative_url }})'s statement is generally more direct and less ambiguous about the outcome of the search. Rather than emphasising moving lights within the forest, he described the group gradually realising that the apparent source lay beyond the trees.

According to Cabansag, the investigators initially believed the lights were somewhere inside the forest because they could see a glow in the direction of a beacon. As they advanced, however, the expected object never appeared. Instead, they reached a point from which they concluded that the light came from much farther away. He wrote that they discovered a lit farmhouse and ultimately determined that "what we were chasing was only a [beacon light]({{ 'beacon-light/' | relative_url }}) off in the distance."<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thecoldfile.com/articles/1980-rendlesham-forest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thecoldfile.com">[The Cold File]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thecoldfile.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1980 rendlesham forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Cold FileLights in the Pines: The 1980 Rendlesham Forest Incident &#124; The Cold File...</span></span></span>

Unlike later narratives that focus on an extraordinary close encounter, Cabansag's surviving contemporary statement does not describe examining or approaching a landed craft. Instead, it records a process of correcting an initial misjudgement about distance as better viewing conditions became available. Ian Ridpath notes that Burroughs and Cabansag broadly agree on this sequence even if they estimated the distance travelled differently.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## How position and distance shaped each report

The contrast between the two accounts is less about one witness affirming a UFO while the other denied one, and more about how each described an evolving visual experience.

Several factors help explain why their narratives differ in emphasis:

* **Changing sightlines:** Dense woodland repeatedly obscured the apparent source, making it difficult to judge both direction and distance until the search reached open ground.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>
* **Perspective:** Burroughs devoted more attention to the confusing sequence of lights encountered during the approach, whereas Cabansag concentrated on the eventual realisation that the principal beacon lay well beyond the forest.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>
