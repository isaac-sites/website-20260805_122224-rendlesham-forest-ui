## Introduction

The [radiation readings]({{ 'radiation/' | relative_url }}) recorded during Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt's night investigation are among the most frequently cited pieces of physical evidence in the Rendlesham Forest UFO incident. However, the contemporaneous audio recording, the later official memorandum, and subsequent technical assessments do not all describe the measurements in the same way. The available evidence shows that Halt's team did detect small fluctuations with a military radiation survey meter while examining three shallow [ground depressions]({{ 'ground-marks/' | relative_url }}) associated with the earlier sighting. What remains disputed is whether those fluctuations represented anything scientifically unusual. Most technical evaluations conclude that the readings were low, inconsistently distributed, and obtained with equipment not designed for precise environmental background surveys, making them difficult to interpret as evidence of an extraordinary event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[ianridpath.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

## What readings Halt dictated at the site

During the early hours of 28 December 1980, Halt's party inspected three shallow depressions in a clearing and used an AN/PDR-27 military radiac meter to look for beta and gamma radiation. As the recorder ran, Halt dictated the team's observations while moving the detector around the depressions, between them and on nearby trees.

The tape does not describe a uniformly radioactive area. Instead, it records mostly weak responses, with readings that hovered around approximately 0.03–0.04 milliroentgens per hour in some locations and brief increases to roughly 0.07 milliroentgens per hour at others. One depression produced only minimal response, while another generated a short-lived increase rather than a sustained elevated reading. [The meter]({{ 'the-meter/' | relative_url }}) was also used at points outside the three depressions, where comparable minor fluctuations were reported.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wikidisc.org/wiki/Rendlesham_Forest_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wikidisc.org">[WikiDisc]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wikidisc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Wiki Disc Rendlesham Forest Incident &#124; Wiki Disc</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wiki Disc Rendlesham Forest Incident &#124; Wiki Disc</span></span></span>

An important distinction emerges when the tape is compared with Halt's memorandum written about two weeks later. The memorandum states that "beta/gamma readings of 0.1 milliroentgens" were recorded, with peak readings in the depressions and near the centre of the triangle they formed, and that a nearby tree showed readings of 0.05–0.07 milliroentgens on the side facing the depressions. The real-time recording is less clear-cut and does not consistently support the later impression that the strongest readings were concentrated at the alleged landing site.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

This difference matters because the tape captures observations as they occurred, whereas the memorandum is a retrospective summary written from memory after the event.

## Why the measurements were difficult to interpret

Several factors limit the evidential value of the recorded radiation measurements.

First, the instrument itself was designed primarily as a military survey meter for detecting relatively high radiation fields associated with nuclear accidents or military operations. It was not intended to perform sensitive environmental mapping of naturally occurring background radiation. Technical assessments conducted after the incident, including consultation with the instrument's manufacturer reported by researchers, concluded that readings at the lower end of its scale were of limited significance and close to the instrument's detection threshold.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wikidisc.org/wiki/Rendlesham_Forest_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wikidisc.org">[WikiDisc]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wikidisc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Wiki Disc Rendlesham Forest Incident &#124; Wiki Disc</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wiki Disc Rendlesham Forest Incident &#124; Wiki Disc</span></span></span>

Second, the recorded increases were small and transient. Rather than showing a clearly defined hotspot, the meter produced occasional spikes that were not consistently confined to the three depressions. Without repeated measurements, it is impossible to determine whether these represented genuine local variation, instrument noise, differences in detector orientation, or ordinary fluctuations in natural background radiation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wikidisc.org/wiki/Rendlesham_Forest_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wikidisc.org">[WikiDisc]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wikidisc.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Wiki Disc Rendlesham Forest Incident &#124; Wiki Disc</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wiki Disc Rendlesham Forest Incident &#124; Wiki Disc</span></span></span>

Third, there was no systematic comparison with unaffected areas before the survey began. Environmental radiation naturally varies with soil composition, moisture, vegetation and geology. Small changes in detector position or height above the ground can also influence readings, especially when using relatively insensitive field equipment.

For these reasons, even researchers who regard the Halt tape as an important historical document generally distinguish between the fact that measurements were taken and the much stronger claim that those measurements demonstrated an anomalous source of radiation.

## Why the later claims became controversial

As the Rendlesham incident gained international attention, the radiation readings were often simplified into claims that the landing site exhibited radiation "ten times background" or that abnormal contamination had been discovered.

Those descriptions rely primarily on the wording of Halt's later memorandum rather than the detailed sequence preserved on the audio tape. Careful comparison of the two records has led several investigators to argue that the memorandum overstates the consistency and location of the meter responses. The tape documents a more ambiguous survey in which readings varied from place to place and did not establish a persistent radioactive centre within the triangle of ground marks.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

The British Ministry of Defence never produced an official scientific assessment of the reported measurements. When questioned years later in Parliament, the government stated that no official evaluation of the radiation readings had been carried out because the incident had not been considered significant for defence purposes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://hansard.parliament.uk/lords/1997-10-14/debates/96394ce5-f0db-4448-af50-43a69fbf3c57/LieutenantColonelCharlesHaltMemorandum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hansard.parliament.uk">[Hansard]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hansard.parliament.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hansard Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt: Memorandum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt: Memorandum - Hansard - UK Parliament...</span></span></span>

## What a controlled survey would have required

From a scientific perspective, the observations would have been far more persuasive had they followed a structured survey protocol.

A controlled investigation would normally include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* A calibrated instrument with documented calibration records.
* Multiple background measurements taken well away from the alleged landing site before examining the depressions.
* Repeated measurements at each location to determine whether readings were reproducible.
* Precise mapping of detector positions, heights and orientations.
* Independent measurements by additional operators using separate instruments.
* Collection and laboratory analysis of soil, vegetation and tree samples.
* Follow-up surveys over subsequent days or weeks to determine whether any anomaly persisted.

</div>

None of these procedures is documented for Halt's field examination. The investigation was an operational military inspection conducted at night rather than a planned scientific study, and the available record reflects those circumstances.

## What the radiation evidence ultimately shows

The radiation readings remain an authentic part of Halt's contemporaneous investigation, but they do not constitute strong standalone physical evidence that an unidentified craft landed in Rendlesham Forest.

The strongest evidence supports three limited conclusions. Halt's team did use a military radiation meter; they observed small fluctuations while examining the alleged landing site; and those observations were considered noteworthy enough to be included in both the tape and the subsequent memorandum. Beyond that, the measurements cannot establish the cause of the fluctuations because they lacked an adequate baseline, systematic controls, repeat testing and laboratory follow-up.     if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
