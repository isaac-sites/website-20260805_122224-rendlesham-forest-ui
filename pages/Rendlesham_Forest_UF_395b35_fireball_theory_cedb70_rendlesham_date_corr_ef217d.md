## Introduction

One of the most significant developments in the debate over the first night of the Rendlesham Forest incident did not come from a new witness or newly released government file. It came from correcting a date. For years, Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s widely cited memorandum placed the initial alarm on **27 December 1980**, making it difficult to connect the sighting with any independently recorded astronomical event. Once investigators established that the first alarm actually occurred in the early hours of **26 December**, an unusually bright fireball reported across southern England emerged as a close chronological match. That correction did not prove the [fireball theory]({{ 'fireball/' | relative_url }}), but it transformed it from speculation into a hypothesis supported by an independent contemporary observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham1b.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span></span></span>

## How the memorandum dated the event

The principal official document describing the incident is Charles Halt's memorandum of 13 January 1981, written roughly two weeks after the events. In its opening paragraph, it states that security police observed unusual lights descending into Rendlesham Forest during the early hours of **27 December 1980**.

Because the memorandum became the most widely circulated official account of the case, that date was repeated in books, articles and television documentaries for many years. Researchers searching for conventional explanations naturally looked for aircraft accidents, astronomical events and weather phenomena occurring during the early hours of 27 December. The absence of a convincing astronomical match on that date appeared to weaken meteor-based explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

The problem was compounded by the memo's influence. Rather than serving as one document among many, it effectively became the chronological framework for later discussions. If its calendar date was wrong, every attempt to compare the incident with independent records also began from the wrong starting point.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

## What police and witness records corrected

Subsequent examination of contemporaneous evidence showed that Halt's memorandum contained a one-day dating error.

Suffolk [Police records]({{ 'police-record/' | relative_url }}), together with the sequence of [witness statements]({{ 'witness-dates/' | relative_url }}) and military events, demonstrated that the first alarm actually occurred during the early hours of **26 December 1980**. Halt's own expedition into the forest likewise shifted from the memorandum's stated date of 29 December to the early hours of **28 December**. In other words, both principal dates in the memorandum appear to have been displaced by approximately one day.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.thecoldfile.com/articles/1980-rendlesham-forest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thecoldfile.com">[The Cold File]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thecoldfile.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1980 rendlesham forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Cold FileLights in the Pines: The 1980 Rendlesham Forest Incident &#124; The Cold File...</span></span></span>

Importantly, this correction is not based solely on sceptical reinterpretation. Even discussions of the memorandum itself acknowledge that the dates require amendment, and the corrected chronology has become the standard timeline used by most modern investigators regardless of their conclusions about what was seen.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

The correction therefore concerns the reliability of the timeline rather than the sincerity of the witnesses. Halt wrote the memorandum retrospectively, not as a contemporaneous log, and later acknowledged that the dates were incorrect.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

## Why the revised date matters

Correcting the first alarm to 26 December immediately changed the search for independent [corroboration]({{ 'corroboration/' | relative_url }}).

Astronomy writer Ian Ridpath, who had previously been unable to find any significant meteor reports matching the memorandum's original date, revisited the question using the corrected chronology. He contacted Dr John Mason of the British Astronomical Association's Meteor Section, which collected reports of unusually bright meteors from observers around Britain. Mason identified an exceptionally bright fireball reported shortly before **03:00 on 26 December 1980**, with contemporary summaries placing it at approximately **02:50**.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham1b.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span></span></span>

Descriptions collected from observers characterised the object as exceptionally brilliant, with brightness approaching that of the full Moon and visible across a wide area of southern England. Such fireballs are well known to produce the illusion that they are descending nearby, particularly when they disappear beyond the horizon or behind trees.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham1b.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span></span></span>
