## Introduction

Who actually led the first security patrol into Rendlesham Forest is less controversial than what the patrol later claimed to have seen. The available evidence indicates that the response developed in stages rather than as a single organised mission. John [Burroughs]({{ 'burroughs/' | relative_url }}) and Staff Sergeant Bud Steffens were the first security personnel to investigate reports of unusual lights beyond RAF [Woodbridge]({{ 'woodbridge/' | relative_url }})'s East Gate. After they reported back, Staff Sergeant Jim Penniston arrived, took charge as the senior non-commissioned officer on the scene, and led the group that continued the search into the forest. Airman First Class Edward Cabansag accompanied Penniston, while Steffens remained behind near the gate. Understanding these changing roles is important because the later public narrative often compresses several phases of the response into a single patrol, obscuring who actually directed the investigation at each point.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## Burroughs and Steffens at the East Gate

The first response originated with personnel already on duty near RAF Woodbridge's East Gate. John Burroughs and Bud Steffens observed unusual coloured lights beyond the perimeter fence and initially approached them believing they might indicate an aircraft accident. That assumption reflected standard security practice during the Cold War: [unexplained lights]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }}) near an active military airfield demanded immediate investigation.

After driving towards the lights, Burroughs and Steffens concluded that the situation required additional personnel. Rather than pressing further into unfamiliar woodland alone, they returned to the gate and requested assistance. This early phase is significant because neither man was acting as commander of a formal investigation. They were the first observers and the first responders, but not the senior decision-makers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781250063311/encounterinrendleshamforest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: us.macmillan.com">[Macmillan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">us.macmillan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Encounter in Rendlesham Forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Encounter in Rendlesham Forest...</span></span></span>

Several later retellings simplify the story by presenting Penniston, Burroughs and [Cabansag]({{ 'cabansag/' | relative_url }}) as though they had entered the forest together from the outset. Contemporary statements and later reconstructions instead indicate that Burroughs and Steffens had already begun investigating before Penniston reached the scene.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781250063311/encounterinrendleshamforest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: us.macmillan.com">[Macmillan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">us.macmillan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Encounter in Rendlesham Forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Encounter in Rendlesham Forest...</span></span></span>

## Penniston's arrival and command role

When Staff Sergeant Jim Penniston arrived, he became the operational leader of the patrol because he outranked the others present. Multiple accounts describe Burroughs and Steffens briefing him on what they had observed before any further advance into the forest.

Penniston initially treated the incident as a possible aircraft crash rather than an encounter with an unidentified craft. He contacted Central Security Control, reported the developing situation and sought permission to continue the investigation. His actions reflected established security procedures: report the suspected emergency, maintain communications and investigate under command authority.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781250063311/encounterinrendleshamforest/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: us.macmillan.com">[Macmillan]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">us.macmillan.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Encounter in Rendlesham Forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Encounter in Rendlesham Forest...</span></span></span>

Once Penniston assumed control, the patrol's composition changed:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* John Burroughs continued into the forest with Penniston.
* Edward Cabansag accompanied them after arriving with Penniston.
* Bud Steffens remained nearer the East Gate rather than advancing with the search party.

This distinction matters because many later disagreements concern events that allegedly occurred after Steffens had ceased accompanying the patrol. As a result, his testimony addresses the initial sighting rather than the later close-range claims associated with Penniston.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

Although Penniston exercised leadership on the ground, he was not operating independently. Master Sergeant J. D. Chandler remained in radio contact from the security control chain and relayed information back to Central Security Control, while Lieutenant Fred Buran, the shift commander, authorised and supervised the overall response from headquarters. This means that Penniston commanded the field investigation, but not the wider security operation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## Cabansag's place in the investigation

Edward Cabansag is sometimes overlooked because later public attention focused on Penniston and Burroughs. His contemporary statement nevertheless provides an important account of the patrol's movements.

Cabansag joined the investigation after Penniston's arrival and accompanied the patrol beyond the vehicle access point into the forest. His description broadly matches Burroughs's early account: the group pursued what appeared to be distant lights, eventually identifying a rotating beacon consistent with the Orford Ness [lighthouse]({{ 'lighthouse/' | relative_url }}), while also reporting other intermittent lights that were harder to explain. Unlike Penniston's later narrative, Cabansag's statement does not describe standing beside a landed triangular craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

Because Cabansag occupied the same patrol as Penniston and Burroughs during the critical period, historians and investigators frequently compare the three statements. The differences between them concern not who led the patrol but what happened after Penniston assumed command. That makes Cabansag's testimony particularly valuable for reconstructing the patrol's movements independently of later claims.

## Why leadership became part of the later debate

The question of leadership became intertwined with the broader controversy because Penniston was both the patrol commander and the witness who later advanced the most detailed account of a landed craft. Some later publications therefore implied that command authority strengthened the credibility of every aspect of his recollection.

The documentary record, however, separates two issues. It consistently supports the conclusion that Penniston led the field patrol after arriving at the East Gate. It does not show equal agreement among participants about what the patrol ultimately encountered. Burroughs's and Cabansag's earliest statements describe following unusual lights through difficult woodland and eventually recognising the lighthouse as one important source of illumination, whereas Penniston's statement concentrated on an apparent structured object and omitted the lighthouse explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>
