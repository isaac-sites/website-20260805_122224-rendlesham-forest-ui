## Introduction

The dispute over the first date in Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s memorandum is not resolved by the memorandum itself but by comparing it with records created much closer to the incident. While Halt's 13 January 1981 summary placed the first security patrol investigation in the early hours of **27 December 1980**, surviving witness statements and duty-related accounts consistently point to the early hours of **26 December**. That distinction matters because contemporaneous testimony generally carries greater evidential weight than a retrospective summary when reconstructing chronology. It does not determine what the witnesses actually saw, but it does help establish when they saw it and therefore which independent records—such as police attendance, duty rosters and other documentation—can legitimately be compared with the event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## Which date did the first witnesses give?

The surviving [first-night]({{ 'first-night/' | relative_url }}) statements were produced before the Rendlesham story had developed into an internationally known UFO case. They were written as operational witness accounts rather than memoirs and, taken together, consistently relate to the incident that occurred during the night of **25–26 December 1980**, not the night of 26–27 December.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

This does not mean every document explicitly writes "26 December" beside every observation. Some statements are undated or refer simply to "that night". However, when they are matched with the known duty sequence and the personnel involved, historians are able to identify them as accounts of the Boxing Day incident rather than a later investigation. The resulting chronology agrees with [police records]({{ 'police-record/' | relative_url }}) and later archival reconstructions far better than the date used in Halt's memorandum.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## Cabansag's dated duty account

Among the first-night participants, Airman Edward [Cabansag]({{ 'cabansag/' | relative_url }}) is particularly important because his account remained comparatively restrained and closely tied to his operational role. His statement describes the patrol following what initially appeared to be unusual lights before concluding that they had been pursuing a distant beacon, later recognised as the [lighthouse]({{ 'lighthouse/' | relative_url }}) beyond the forest. Rather than introducing dramatic later claims, his statement concentrates on what the patrol actually observed during the duty response.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

Cabansag's evidence is valuable in the chronology debate because it belongs to the immediate documentary record assembled shortly after the incident. His participation on the first response team is independently established, and his account aligns with the accepted sequence of security personnel being dispatched after reports of possible lights or an aircraft incident during the early hours of 26 December. Researchers who have examined the original witness files frequently treat Cabansag's statement as one of the more reliable contemporary descriptions precisely because it remained close to the operational facts rather than later interpretations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## How the surviving statements are catalogued

The surviving collection of first-night statements includes accounts from Lieutenant Fred Buran, John [Burroughs]({{ 'burroughs/' | relative_url }}), Edward Cabansag, Master Sergeant J. D. Chandler and Staff Sergeant Jim Penniston. Most were produced within days of the incident, making them significantly closer in time than Halt's memorandum, which was drafted more than two weeks later as a summary for the Ministry of Defence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

Several features of this collection are important when evaluating dates:

* They represent multiple participants occupying different positions within the security chain of command rather than a single narrator.
* They were created before many of the later public claims and embellishments associated with the case emerged.
* Although some statements are undated or incomplete, they can be placed within the operational sequence through the identities of the witnesses, their duties and the events they describe.
* Collectively, they fit the chronology placing the first forest investigation in the early hours of 26 December rather than 27 December.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## Why testimony nearer the event carries greater weight

Historical research generally gives greater weight to records produced close to an event than to later summaries, especially where chronology is concerned. Immediate witness statements are not infallible: observers may misunderstand what they saw, omit details or disagree with one another. Nevertheless, they are usually less vulnerable to memory drift than documents written weeks, months or years afterwards.

That principle is particularly relevant in the Rendlesham chronology because Halt was not writing a minute-by-minute operational log. His memorandum condensed multiple events for administrative purposes after the incident had ended. A simple one-day calendar error in such a retrospective summary is more plausible than identical dating errors appearing independently across several contemporary operational accounts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>
