## Introduction

The best-known official document in the Rendlesham Forest case is Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s memorandum of 13 January 1981. Because it was written on US Air Force stationery and later released under the US Freedom of Information Act, it has often been treated as a near-contemporaneous account of what happened during the [first night]({{ 'first-night/' | relative_url }}). In reality, that section of the memorandum is a second-hand summary. Halt was not present when Jim Penniston, John Burroughs and the other security police entered the forest on the night of 25–26 December 1980. Instead, he condensed multiple witness reports into a single narrative that presents the encounter as a more coherent observation of a metallic triangular object than the surviving individual statements do.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

Understanding this distinction is important when comparing the earliest evidence with later retellings. The memorandum did not invent the idea of a structured object, but it merged separate observations, uncertainties and differing perspectives into one official account. That process makes the incident easier to describe, yet it also obscures where the witnesses actually agreed and where they did not.

## What the individual statements say

The first-night [witness statements]({{ 'witness-dates/' | relative_url }}) were written independently by several members of the security police involved in the search. Read together, they reveal both overlap and significant variation rather than a single, unified description.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

Penniston's statement is the strongest early source for a structured object. He described approaching what appeared to be a mechanical object illuminated by coloured lights and included sketches. Even so, his original statement was comparatively restrained. It lacks many elements that became famous decades later, including prolonged examination, touching a craft, binary code or telepathic experiences.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

Burroughs' account is noticeably more cautious. He described pursuing unusual lights through the forest but also recorded that the distant flashing beacon they had been following was identified as the Orford Ness [lighthouse]({{ 'lighthouse/' | relative_url }}). His statement still refers to [unexplained lights]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }}) during the incident, yet it does not present a detailed close encounter with a landed craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

Edward Cabansag's statement is more explicit still. He wrote that what they had initially chased appeared to be a [beacon light]({{ 'beacon-light/' | relative_url }}) and that a glowing area resolved into an ordinary illuminated farmhouse as they approached. His account therefore combines unusual observations with an identified conventional light source rather than a confirmed landed vehicle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

J. D. Chandler, who supervised communications during the search, documented Penniston's reports over the radio but did not describe witnessing a craft himself. His statement records the developing situation without confirming many of the stronger claims that later became associated with the incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

Taken together, these statements describe an unusual security response involving coloured lights, changing perceptions of distance and uncertainty about what was being observed. They do not all describe the same object in the same way.

## How Halt summarised the first night

When Halt drafted his memorandum over two weeks later, he compressed those differing reports into a concise official narrative.

Instead of identifying which witness supplied which description, the memorandum states that the patrol "reported seeing a strange glowing object in the forest" and immediately follows this with a single physical description: the object "was described as being metallic in appearance and triangular in shape, approximately two to three metres across the base and approximately two metres high."<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

That wording is significant because it presents the description as though it reflects the patrol collectively. The memorandum does not distinguish between observations made by Penniston, who supplied the strongest structured-object description, and those made by [Burroughs]({{ 'burroughs/' | relative_url }}) or Cabansag, whose surviving statements are more tentative and include references to the lighthouse. Readers therefore encounter one apparently consistent account instead of several partially overlapping ones.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

The memo also omits much of the uncertainty visible in the original documents. There is little indication that some participants believed they had ultimately identified a lighthouse, that different witnesses emphasised different lights, or that the patrol's interpretations evolved as they moved through the forest. Instead, the report flows naturally from unusual lights to a metallic triangular object, creating a cleaner narrative sequence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

This does not necessarily imply deliberate embellishment. Official memoranda often summarise complex events for senior readers rather than preserving every disagreement. Nevertheless, summarisation changes how evidence is perceived. A synthesis can appear stronger than the individual observations from which it was assembled.

## What gets lost when accounts are merged

The principal loss is attribution. Once multiple reports are combined, it becomes difficult to determine which witness actually supported each specific claim.

For example:

* The memorandum's physical description broadly reflects Penniston's account but is presented without identifying him as its primary source.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

## Why the distinction matters

The Halt memorandum remains an important historical document because it records what senior command understood had been reported shortly after the events. It also became the document that introduced the Rendlesham incident to a much wider audience after its [public release]({{ 'public-release/' | relative_url }}) in 1983. Its official status gave the case credibility and helped establish the familiar image of a metallic triangular craft in Rendlesham Forest.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

However, as evidence for the first night's events, the memorandum is best understood as a synthesis rather than an eyewitness statement. Historians and investigators therefore distinguish between the primary observations recorded by the participants themselves and Halt's later administrative summary. That distinction does not determine whether an unusual event occurred, but it does affect how confidently particular details—especially the clear description of a metallic triangular object—can be attributed to the entire patrol rather than to specific witnesses within it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>
