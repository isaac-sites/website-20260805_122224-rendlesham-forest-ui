## Introduction

One of the strongest independent pieces of evidence that Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s forest investigation took place during the early hours of **28 December 1980**, rather than **29 December** as stated in his later memorandum, is a reported telephone call from RAF [Bentwaters]({{ 'bentwaters/' | relative_url }}) to **RAF Watton** at approximately **3.25 am**. Unlike recollections written weeks later, this reported operational log entry was created as part of routine air-defence activity while the incident was unfolding. It therefore serves as an important chronological anchor in debates over the date errors in the official account.

The significance of the call is not that it confirms unidentified objects on [radar]({{ 'radar/' | relative_url }}). In fact, the available evidence indicates the opposite: Watton reportedly found nothing unusual on its radar displays. Its importance lies in showing that Halt's team was actively observing unusual lights at around 3.25 am on **28 December**, placing the events recorded on the famous Halt tape on that date rather than on 29 December.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham1b.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[ianridpath.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span></span></span>

## The reported 3.25 am log entry

RAF Watton was an important Royal Air Force air traffic control and radar centre responsible for monitoring airspace across eastern England. During Halt's investigation, personnel at RAF Bentwaters reportedly contacted Watton to determine whether any unusual aerial activity was visible on military radar.

Research by Nicholas Redfern, later highlighted by Ian Ridpath and supported by Ministry of Defence documentation released through the National [Archives]({{ 'archives/' | relative_url }}), identified a reported [Watton log]({{ 'watton-log/' | relative_url }}) entry recording a telephone call from Bentwaters at approximately **03:25 on 28 December 1980**. According to these accounts:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Bentwaters requested a radar check while Halt's party was observing lights.
* RAF Watton examined its radar picture.
* No unusual radar returns were detected.
* The interaction was recorded in Watton's operational log.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham1b.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[ianridpath.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span></span></span>

</div>

The timing matters because operational logbooks are normally completed as events occur, making them considerably less vulnerable to later memory errors than retrospective summaries.

## How the call aligns with the forest tape

The Halt tape is one of the most valuable contemporaneous records from the incident because it captures observations as they were being made rather than reconstructed afterwards.

Near the latter part of the recording, Halt describes continuing observations of lights before announcing times around **03:30** and later **04:00**. Researchers comparing the tape with the reported RAF Watton log note that the **03:25** telephone call fits naturally into this sequence. During this phase of the investigation, Halt instructed the command post to contact RAF Watton for radar confirmation while his team continued watching lights in the sky. The transcript's timing is therefore consistent with a radar enquiry occurring immediately before the recorded 03:30 commentary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Transcript%20of%20Colonel%20Halt%20Tape%20%28Ian%20Ridpath%29.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avalon Library Transcri</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">pt of Col. Halt’s tape Transcribed bDecember 7, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2019</span></span></span>

This correspondence between an independently reported military log and a contemporaneous audio recording provides a much firmer chronological framework than Halt's memorandum, which was written on 13 January 1981, more than two weeks after the events.

## Why the call supports 28 December rather than 29 December

The value of the Watton record lies in chronology rather than in proving what the witnesses observed.

If the radar enquiry was logged at approximately **03:25 on 28 December**, then the associated observations—and the relevant portion of the Halt tape—must also belong to that same early morning. This creates a direct conflict with Halt's memorandum, which dates his investigation to **29 December**.

The evidence therefore suggests the following sequence:

* Halt's team entered Rendlesham Forest during the night of **27–28 December 1980**.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://videoovni.fr/en/articles/rendlesham-forest-1980-roswell-britannique-militaires-usaf.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: videoovni.fr">[videoovni.fr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">videoovni.fr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">rendlesham forest 1980 roswell britannique militaires usaf</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rendlesham Forest 1980: Britain’s Roswell… &#124; UFO VIDEOMay 28, 2026 — Official military report Incident: December 26–28, 1980 4 min read H...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 28, 2026</span></span></span>

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* While observing lights, Bentwaters contacted RAF Watton at approximately **03:25**.
* The observations continued beyond **03:30**, as heard on the tape.
* Halt later prepared his memorandum but advanced the calendar date by one day.

</div>

## What the radar check did—and did not—show

The RAF Watton call is sometimes misunderstood as evidence that radar tracked an unidentified object. The available documentary record does not support that interpretation.

Accounts from Ministry of Defence files, together with later interviews with personnel connected to RAF Watton, consistently state that controllers checked their radar displays and **found nothing unusual**. Former duty commander Squadron Leader Derek Coumbe later recalled receiving the request from Bentwaters and stated that repeated checks failed to reveal any unidentified targets despite continued requests from the American personnel.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufos-scientificresearch.blogspot.com/2013/10/did-radar-track-rendlesham-forest-uap_15.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufos-scientificresearch.blogspot.com">[UFOs Scientific Research]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufos-scientificresearch.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFOs Scientific Research Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFOs Scientific ResearchUnidentified Anomalous Phenomena - scientific research: Did radar track the Rendlesham Forest UAP? Consequently, the call provides two distinct pieces of evidence:

* **Chronological evidence:** it supports dating Halt's observations to the early hours of 28 December.
* **Operational evidence:** it indicates that the British radar operators did not detect corresponding unidentified radar contacts.

These are separate questions. The absence of radar returns neither proves nor disproves what witnesses believed they were seeing visually.

## What remains uncertain about the published log

Although the reported Watton log has become an important reference point in Rendlesham research, some limitations remain.

The published discussions generally describe the contents of the operational log rather than reproducing the complete original page in full context. Researchers therefore rely on Ministry of Defence correspondence, later investigative work, and corroborating testimony rather than on a widely available facsimile of the entire logbook entry.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufotransparency.com/files/intl-uk-rendlesham-defe-24-1948-defe-24-1948-rendlesham-forest-file" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufotransparency.com">[UFO Transparency]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufotransparency.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO TransparencyRendlesham Forest Incident, DEFE 24/1948 (Halt Memo, Suffolk Constabulary correspondence, parliamentary briefings), UK Mi...</span></span></span>

There is also no indication that the log recorded detailed descriptions of the lights themselves. Its operational purpose was simply to document that a radar enquiry had been received and that no unusual contacts were observed.

    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
