## Introduction

Edward Cabansag's contemporary statement ends with one of the most important yet often overlooked observations from the [first night]({{ 'first-night/' | relative_url }}) of the Rendlesham Forest incident: after pursuing mysterious lights through the woods, he concluded that what remained was "only a beacon light off in the distance." That apparently simple phrase has become central to the debate over whether the patrol ultimately identified the source of the lights as the Orfordness [lighthouse]({{ 'lighthouse/' | relative_url }}) or whether Cabansag was referring to some other fixed coastal beacon. The distinction matters because it bears directly on how the first night's observations should be interpreted. While later accounts emphasised extraordinary close encounters, Cabansag's earliest known statement instead describes a search in which initially puzzling lights gradually resolved into ordinary distant light sources. The surviving evidence strongly suggests that the beacon was the Orfordness lighthouse, but the wording of Cabansag's statement leaves enough ambiguity that absolute identification cannot be claimed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-the-truth-about-rendlesham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: The Truth about Rendlesham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: The Truth about Rendlesham - The SkepticApril 2, 2006...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 2, 2006</span></span></span>

## Cabansag's Exact Description of the Beacon

[Cabansag]({{ 'cabansag/' | relative_url }})'s statement describes a sequence rather than a single observation. He explained that the patrol initially believed the lights were somewhere within or immediately beyond the forest. As they advanced, however, the apparent location of the lights changed.

His account states that:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* they realised the lights seemed to come from beyond the forest rather than inside it;
* a glow that appeared close to the beacon proved to be an illuminated farmhouse when they approached;
* after reaching a better viewpoint, they concluded that "what we were chasing was only a beacon light off in the distance."<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.creativespirits.net/rendlesham-forest-incident/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: creativespirits.net">[Creative Spirits]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">creativespirits.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Creative Spirits Rendlesham Forest incident &#124; Creativespirits.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Creative SpiritsRendlesham Forest incident &#124; Creativespirits.netFebruary 21, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 21, 2019</span></span></span>

That wording is significant because Cabansag does not describe discovering a landed object hidden among the trees. Instead, his narrative ends with the patrol recognising that the remaining [unexplained]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }}) light was much farther away than they had first believed.

Importantly, he never explicitly names the beacon. Unlike later commentators, he simply uses the generic expression "beacon light," leaving modern readers to determine which fixed light best matches his description.

## Comparing the Possible Light Sources

The phrase "beacon light" has generated discussion because several kinds of lights existed in the general area, although not all fit Cabansag's description equally well.

### The Orfordness Lighthouse

The Orfordness lighthouse is the leading candidate for several reasons.

First, another member of the same patrol, John [Burroughs]({{ 'burroughs/' | relative_url }}), gave a closely related account. Burroughs wrote that the patrol followed a rotating beacon for roughly two miles before realising it "was coming from a lighthouse." The similarity between the two statements strongly suggests that both men were describing the same final stage of the search rather than two separate lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.creativespirits.net/rendlesham-forest-incident/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: creativespirits.net">[Creative Spirits]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">creativespirits.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Creative Spirits Rendlesham Forest incident &#124; Creativespirits.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Creative SpiritsRendlesham Forest incident &#124; Creativespirits.netFebruary 21, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 21, 2019</span></span></span>

Second, later analysis noted that the direction of the observed flashing matched the bearing of the Orfordness lighthouse from Rendlesham Forest. During [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s separate investigation two nights later, his tape recording captured a flashing light with an interval of roughly five seconds—consistent with the lighthouse's flash cycle at the time. Although Halt rejected the lighthouse explanation for everything he witnessed, the timing correspondence became one of the strongest conventional explanations for at least some of the observed lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendlesham_Forest_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rendlesham Forest incident</span></span></span>

Third, Cabansag's description of first seeing a glow near the beacon and then discovering it to be a lit farmhouse fits a scenario in which a distant lighthouse remained visible beyond nearer rural buildings as the patrol crossed fields toward the forest edge.

### Other Fixed Lights

Some sceptical discussions have also mentioned other navigational lights, including maritime beacons and the former Shipwash lightship, because multiple coastal navigation aids were visible from parts of the Suffolk coast under suitable conditions. These possibilities illustrate that "beacon light" was not necessarily a technical reference to a lighthouse alone. However, no surviving first-night statement specifically identifies one of these alternative navigation lights as the object Cabansag observed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-the-truth-about-rendlesham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: The Truth about Rendlesham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: The Truth about Rendlesham - The SkepticApril 2, 2006...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 2, 2006</span></span></span>

Likewise, the illuminated farmhouse mentioned in Cabansag's statement clearly cannot itself be the final beacon. His account distinguishes between the farmhouse, which was identified at closer range, and the more distant beacon that remained visible beyond it.

## Why Cabansag Did Not Simply Say "Lighthouse"

One of the small but intriguing differences between the witness statements is the language each witness chose.

Cabansag consistently referred to a "beacon light."

Burroughs explicitly concluded that the beacon came from "a lighthouse."

That difference may have several ordinary explanations:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--caution" markdown="1">

* Cabansag may simply have been describing what he saw rather than identifying the structure producing it.
* He may not have personally confirmed the source visually, even if others believed it to be a lighthouse.
* The statement may reflect caution, recording only what he was confident about instead of making a stronger identification.

</div>

Nothing in the surviving statement explains why he selected the more general term. Consequently, historians cannot confidently infer that he meant a different object merely because he used different wording.

## Why the Identification Remains Partly Uncertain

Despite the strength of the lighthouse interpretation, some uncertainty remains.

The principal limitation is that Cabansag never wrote, "the beacon was the Orfordness lighthouse." That specific identification comes instead from comparison with Burroughs' statement, geographical reconstruction and later analyses of the sight lines rather than from Cabansag's own words.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.creativespirits.net/rendlesham-forest-incident/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: creativespirits.net">[Creative Spirits]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">creativespirits.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Creative Spirits Rendlesham Forest incident &#124; Creativespirits.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Creative SpiritsRendlesham Forest incident &#124; Creativespirits.netFebruary 21, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 21, 2019</span></span></span>

There are additional reasons for caution:

* the surviving statement is apparently undated, although generally regarded as contemporary;
* Cabansag later reportedly said he signed the statement under pressure, introducing questions about its preparation without establishing that any particular passage was inaccurate;
* later witness recollections evolved over decades, making the earliest statements especially valuable but not automatically complete.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-the-truth-about-rendlesham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: The Truth about Rendlesham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: The Truth about Rendlesham - The SkepticApril 2, 2006...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 2, 2006</span></span></span>

## What the Beacon Means for Interpreting Cabansag's Account

The importance of Cabansag's beacon is less about proving a lighthouse explanation for the entire Rendlesham incident than about understanding his own testimony.

His statement shows an investigation that began with genuinely confusing coloured lights but ended with increasing confidence that the remaining visible light lay beyond the forest. That progression differs sharply from narratives in which the patrol conclusively encountered an extraordinary craft at close range.

Because Burroughs independently described following a beacon until recognising it as a lighthouse, the two earliest accounts reinforce one another on this specific point, even though they differ from later, more dramatic retellings. At the same time, Cabansag's choice of the generic phrase "beacon light" rather than naming the Orfordness lighthouse prevents the evidence from being entirely conclusive. The result is a measured historical assessment: the available contemporary evidence strongly favours the Orfordness lighthouse as the beacon Cabansag described, but the wording of his own statement leaves a small degree of uncertainty that cannot be eliminated from the surviving record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-the-truth-about-rendlesham/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[skeptic.org.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: The Truth about Rendlesham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: The Truth about Rendlesham - The SkepticApril 2, 2006...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 2, 2006</span></span></span>

    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
