## Introduction

The fact that Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s memorandum survives in the British Ministry of Defence archive is important, but its significance is often overstated. The archive establishes that the memorandum was an authentic government record that entered official Ministry correspondence through normal administrative channels. It confirms provenance: who produced the document, when it was received, and that officials retained it as part of the government's records. It does **not** establish that every reported observation in the memorandum was factually accurate, nor does it prove that the reported lights represented an unidentified craft.

Understanding this distinction is essential when assessing the Rendlesham Forest incident. The archive proves the existence and official handling of the document itself. The truth of the observations described within it remains a separate historical question requiring evaluation alongside witness testimony, later investigations and competing explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/ufo-research-guide-2013.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo research guide 2013</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesResearch Notes 6August 11, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2014</span></span></span>

## How the memorandum entered the Ministry record

The Halt memorandum, dated 13 January 1981 and headed **"[Unexplained Lights]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }})"**, was prepared by Lieutenant Colonel Charles I. Halt, Deputy Base Commander at RAF [Woodbridge]({{ 'woodbridge/' | relative_url }}). It was transmitted to the Ministry of Defence through Defence Secretariat 8 (DS8), the branch responsible for handling reports of unidentified aerial phenomena and related correspondence at the time. This administrative route placed the document within official defence files rather than leaving it as an internal United States Air Force memorandum.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/ufo-research-guide-2013.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo research guide 2013</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesResearch Notes 6August 11, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2014</span></span></span>

Its later archival history is equally well documented.

The original report was preserved in Ministry files, and a dedicated Rendlesham file was subsequently created after the incident attracted public and parliamentary attention. When the Ministry progressively released its historical UFO records under public access rules and later transferred them to The National [Archives]({{ 'archives/' | relative_url }}), Halt's memorandum formed the opening document in file **DEFE 24/1948**, where it remains part of the surviving government record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/aug-2009-highlights-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Highlights Guide</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Archives Highlights Guide</span></span></span>

## What archival cataloguing confirms about provenance

Archival preservation serves a different purpose from scientific verification. An archive records the history of documents, not necessarily the truth of every statement they contain.

Because the memorandum survives within Ministry files, researchers can verify its provenance through several independent characteristics:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* its official date;
* its author;
* its subject heading;
* its place within Ministry correspondence;
* its relationship to later internal briefings and parliamentary responses.

</div>

These features make the memorandum an authentic historical document rather than an anonymous or disputed text. They also allow scholars to reconstruct how the British government processed information about the Rendlesham reports over subsequent years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/ufo-research-guide-2013.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo research guide 2013</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesResearch Notes 6August 11, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2014</span></span></span>

The surrounding file strengthens this context. DEFE 24/1948 does not contain only Halt's report. It also includes later Ministry correspondence, responses to members of the public, parliamentary briefing material and internal discussion extending into the 1990s. That broader documentary context demonstrates continuing official interest in responding to public enquiries, even though the Ministry did not regard the incident as a defence threat.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/aug-2009-highlights-guide.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Highlights Guide</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Archives Highlights Guide</span></span></span>

## What an official file cannot verify about the sightings

An archived government document is evidence that officials received information—not that they endorsed every claim within it.

The memorandum contains observations from multiple individuals, including reports of:

* unusual lights;
* an apparently triangular object;
* ground impressions;
* radiation measurements;
* [moving lights]({{ 'moving-lights/' | relative_url }}) observed during Halt's later expedition.

The archive confirms that these claims were reported. It does not independently confirm that each event occurred exactly as described, nor that witnesses interpreted what they saw correctly. Those questions require corroborating evidence beyond the memorandum itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/ufo-research-guide-2013.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo research guide 2013</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesResearch Notes 6August 11, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2014</span></span></span>

## The Ministry's handling shows receipt, not endorsement

One of the strongest pieces of evidence against treating the archive as official validation is the Ministry's own documented response.

Parliamentary answers and later Ministry briefings consistently stated that the memorandum had been assessed by officials responsible for air defence. ## Why provenance still matters

Although archival preservation does not prove extraordinary claims, it substantially increases the memorandum's historical value.

Without an authenticated government copy, researchers would have to rely on recollections, privately circulated reproductions or later retellings. Because the memorandum exists within an [official file]({{ 'official-file/' | relative_url }}) whose provenance is documented, historians can distinguish between:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the wording actually submitted to the Ministry in January 1981;
* later witness recollections;
* subsequent interpretations added during decades of public debate.

</div>

This allows the memorandum to serve as a stable contemporary source against which later claims can be compared. Discrepancies—such as the memorandum's one-day dating errors or differences between the report and later witness accounts—can be identified precisely because the archival record preserves the original document rather than only later narratives.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/ufo-research-guide-2013.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo research guide 2013</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesResearch Notes 6August 11, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2014</span></span></span>

## The key historical takeaway

The official archive proves a limited but significant set of facts. It establishes that Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt wrote an official memorandum, that it entered Ministry of Defence records through recognised administrative channels, and that the document has been preserved within the government's archival collection. It also documents how British officials handled and later assessed the report.

What the archive does **not** prove is that every observation described in the memorandum occurred exactly as reported, that an unidentified craft was present in Rendlesham Forest, or that the Ministry of Defence accepted the witnesses' interpretation of the events. The archive authenticates the document's provenance and administrative history; it does not convert reported observations into established historical fact.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/ufo-research-guide-2013.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo research guide 2013</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesResearch Notes 6August 11, 2014...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2014</span></span></span>

    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
