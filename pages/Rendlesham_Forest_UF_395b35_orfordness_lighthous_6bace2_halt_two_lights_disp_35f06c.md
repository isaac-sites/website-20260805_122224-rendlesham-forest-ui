## Introduction

One of the strongest objections to the Orfordness Lighthouse explanation is Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s later insistence that he did **not** mistake the lighthouse for the unidentified light. Instead, Halt has repeatedly argued that he recognised the lighthouse as a familiar, fixed reference and simultaneously observed a separate, [unexplained]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }}) light elsewhere. If correct, this would significantly weaken the argument that the principal light recorded during the Rendlesham Forest incident was simply the lighthouse.

The difficulty is that this claim rests largely on Halt's later recollections rather than on the contemporaneous evidence. His audio recording from the night, the bearings recorded during the investigation, and later reconstructions have led many researchers to conclude that the separation between the two lights is either much smaller than Halt remembered or cannot be demonstrated from the available evidence. As a result, the "two lights" question remains one of the central unresolved disputes in the [lighthouse]({{ 'lighthouse/' | relative_url }}) debate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/Halt_affidavit.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span></span></span>

## Halt's later account of separate sources

In interviews, lectures and a sworn affidavit produced decades after the incident, Halt maintained that the Orfordness Lighthouse was visible throughout the investigation but lay well to the right of the unidentified object. According to this later version, he and the other personnel knew exactly where the lighthouse stood and deliberately distinguished it from the mysterious light they were following.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/Halt_affidavit.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span></span></span>

This argument is important because it answers the sceptical explanation directly. Rather than denying the lighthouse's existence, Halt accepts that it was visible but contends that it was merely a background landmark while another object behaved independently.

Supporters of Halt's interpretation argue that experienced military observers were unlikely to confuse a familiar coastal navigation light with an anomalous object for an extended period. They also note that Halt never abandoned this position despite decades of criticism.

However, critics point out that this explicit "two separate lights" formulation became much more prominent years after the event than it was in the original documentation. Neither Halt's contemporaneous tape recording nor his official memorandum identifies the lighthouse as a separate object that was consciously excluded from consideration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/Halt_affidavit.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span></span></span>

## What the contemporaneous tape does and does not show

The strongest primary evidence from the investigation is the audio cassette Halt recorded while leading the patrol through the forest.

On the tape, the party repeatedly comments on a flashing light ahead, estimating bearings generally around 110–120 degrees and describing it as lying near the coast or horizon. The timing of the audible flashes closely matches the Orfordness Lighthouse's five-second rotation, one of the principal reasons sceptical investigators identify it as the same object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendlesham_Forest_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rendlesham Forest incident</span></span></span>

The recording does contain moments where observers refer to more than one light. At one stage someone remarks that there are "two lights", which has been cited by proponents of Halt's later interpretation. Yet the tape alone does not establish that one of these lights was definitely the lighthouse and the other an unrelated phenomenon. It records the observation but provides insufficient positional information to identify both objects with certainty.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://skepticalinquirer.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2019/03/Issue-04-8.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skepticalinquirer.org">[Skeptical Inquirer]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skepticalinquirer.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Skeptical Inquirerthe</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Skeptical Inquirer SPONTANEOUS HUMAN...</span></span></span>

Equally significant is what the tape does **not** contain. It does not include a clear statement such as, "The lighthouse is over there, and this other light is somewhere else." That distinction appears mainly in Halt's retrospective accounts rather than in the real-time recording.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/Halt_affidavit.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span></span></span>

## Bearing reconstructions and the unresolved dispute

Researchers attempting to reconstruct the patrol's route have compared Halt's recorded compass bearings with maps, photographs and known landmarks.

Ian Ridpath and other sceptical investigators argue that the recorded bearings consistently point towards the direction of Orfordness. From this perspective, the unidentified flashing light and the lighthouse occupy essentially the same line of sight, making Halt's later claim of a large angular separation difficult to reconcile with the recorded evidence. Ridpath has specifically criticised Halt's later assertion that the lighthouse lay as much as 40 degrees to the right, arguing that site geometry does not support such a large offset.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/Halt_affidavit.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span></span></span>

Some therefore argue that modern reconstructions cannot completely exclude the possibility that observers intermittently saw more than one distant light.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.astronomyufo.com/UFO/SUNlite4_5.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: astronomyufo.com">[astronomyufo.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">astronomyufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Shedding some light on UFOlogy and UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">September 22, 2012...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 22, 2012</span></span></span>

## Why the disagreement persists

The disagreement ultimately reflects the different weight given to contemporaneous evidence versus later testimony.

Those who favour the lighthouse explanation place greatest emphasis on the tape recording, the five-second flash rhythm and the reconstructed bearings. From that perspective, Halt's later insistence on two separate lights represents a memory that evolved over time and is less reliable than the evidence created during the event itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/Halt_affidavit.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span></span></span>

Those who reject the lighthouse explanation instead argue that the senior officer leading the investigation was in the best position to distinguish known landmarks from anomalous phenomena. They view Halt's repeated assertion that he saw both the lighthouse and another light as evidence that the conventional explanation is incomplete.

Because the available evidence does not conclusively identify every light visible that night, neither position can entirely eliminate the other's interpretation. The tape confirms that multiple lights were discussed at various moments, but it does not conclusively demonstrate that one was the lighthouse and another an independent unidentified object. Conversely, later testimony clearly asserts that distinction, but it is not fully corroborated by the contemporaneous record. This tension is why Halt's claim of "two separate lights" remains the principal challenge to the Orfordness Lighthouse explanation rather than a settled refutation of it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/Halt_affidavit.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[ianridpath.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Col Halt&#x27;s affidavit</span></span></span>
