## Introduction

One of the most unsettling moments reported by John [Burroughs]({{ 'burroughs/' | relative_url }}) during the [first night]({{ 'first-night/' | relative_url }}) of the Rendlesham Forest incident was not a light in the trees but a terrifying scream from the darkness. In his early witness statement, Burroughs described hearing a sound "like a woman screaming" while the patrol moved through unfamiliar woodland at night. Although the noise contributed to the group's sense that something extraordinary was happening, later analysis has identified a straightforward natural explanation: the alarm call of a muntjac deer. This possibility does not explain every aspect of the Rendlesham incident, but it provides an important example of how unfamiliar sounds, darkness and heightened alertness can amplify uncertainty during a military search.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## How Burroughs described the sound

Burroughs's handwritten statement, made close to the events of December 1980, records more than unusual lights. He also noted "strange noises, like a woman screaming", together with considerable movement in the woods and loud noises from nearby animals. The description appears alongside his account of advancing through dense forest towards flashing lights that the patrol initially struggled to identify.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

The scream is significant because it demonstrates how the patrol experienced the forest as a complete sensory environment rather than simply observing lights. [Visibility]({{ 'visibility/' | relative_url }}) was poor, the men were crossing uneven ground at night, and they were investigating what they initially believed might have been an aircraft crash. In those conditions, an unexpected human-like cry naturally increased tension and may have reinforced the impression that something unusual was unfolding.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## Why a muntjac deer is a plausible explanation

Later investigators pointed to the muntjac deer as a highly credible source of the scream. Muntjac are small deer introduced into Britain that have become well established across much of southern and eastern England, including Suffolk. When alarmed, they produce an abrupt, loud bark or scream that many people unfamiliar with the species mistake for a distressed human voice.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

Several factors make this explanation persuasive:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* **The location fits.** Rendlesham Forest supports populations of deer, including muntjac, making their presence entirely expected.
* **The timing fits.** Muntjac are active during darkness and often vocalise when disturbed.
* **The description fits.** Burroughs compared the sound to a woman screaming, a comparison frequently made by people hearing muntjac calls for the first time.
* **The patrol's experience fits.** Burroughs later acknowledged that he had never previously been in those woods, meaning unfamiliar wildlife sounds could easily have been startling.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

## The same pattern during Charles Halt's investigation

The importance of the deer explanation increases because similar sounds were reported again two nights later during Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s better-known investigation.

Halt's audio recording refers to loud noises apparently coming from nearby animals. Although Halt initially interpreted the disturbance as coming from farm livestock, later examination of the area suggested there were no barnyard animals exactly where he believed. Researchers instead proposed that the noises almost certainly originated from wildlife within the forest itself, with muntjac deer being a particularly strong candidate.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Transcript%20of%20Colonel%20Halt%20Tape%20%28Ian%20Ridpath%29.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avalon Library Transcri</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">pt of Col. ## How unfamiliar sounds shaped the patrol

The scream mattered because it affected the patrol's interpretation of everything else they encountered.

Military security personnel are trained observers, but training does not eliminate the influence of environmental conditions. On the contrary, searching for a possible crash site at night requires heightened vigilance. Every unexpected stimulus becomes potentially important until it can be identified.

Several psychological and environmental factors were working together:

* Darkness prevented accurate identification of distant sound sources.
* Dense woodland distorted echoes and made judging direction difficult.
* The expectation of finding an aircraft accident encouraged the patrol to treat unusual events seriously.
* Unfamiliar British wildlife produced sounds that American personnel had little experience recognising.
* Stress and divided attention made independent sensory cues appear connected, even when they may have had unrelated causes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>
