## Introduction

The surviving RAF Watton control-room log is the most important contemporary radar-related document connected to Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s observations in the early hours of 28 December 1980. Rather than recording an unidentified object, the log documents a routine operational exchange: RAF [Bentwaters]({{ 'bentwaters/' | relative_url }}) asked Eastern Radar whether any aircraft were in the area, and the controller identified a known piece of civil air traffic designated **UA37**, flying southbound at **Flight Level 370 (37,000 feet)**. The same entry notes that Bentwaters was reporting UFO sightings, but it contains no record of an unidentified radar return corresponding to those reports. This distinction is significant because later claims of radar confirmation have often been presented more broadly than the surviving contemporaneous evidence supports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/rendlesham-forest-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[drdavidclarke.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">December 31, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2020</span></span></span>

## The 03:25 call from Bentwaters

The key log entry is timed **03:25 GMT on 28 December 1980**, during the period when Halt and other USAF personnel were investigating lights in and above Rendlesham Forest.

According to the released RAF Watton log, the sequence was straightforward:

> Bentwaters Command Post contacted Eastern [Radar]({{ 'radar/' | relative_url }}) requesting information on aircraft in the area. The response identified **"UA37 traffic southbound FL370"**, after which the log noted that Bentwaters was reporting UFO sightings and that reporting procedures were being followed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/rendlesham-forest-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[drdavidclarke.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">December 31, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2020</span></span></span>

The entry is operational rather than descriptive. It does not attempt to interpret what witnesses on the ground were seeing, nor does it speculate about possible explanations. Its purpose was simply to record communications between the radar station and Bentwaters.

Equally important is what the entry does **not** contain. There is no notation of:

* an unknown radar target;
* an aircraft manoeuvring unusually;
* a target approaching RAF [Woodbridge]({{ 'woodbridge/' | relative_url }}) or Bentwaters;
* radar operators initiating an interception or alert.

Had an unidentified track been recognised as a genuine air-defence concern, additional operational logging would normally have been expected. No such record has been released.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/rendlesham-forest-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[drdavidclarke.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">December 31, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2020</span></span></span>

## Why UA37 was identified as normal traffic

The designation **UA37** has sometimes been misunderstood in discussions of the Rendlesham incident. It was not the name of a mysterious object but the identifier used for recognised civil traffic using a standard north–south airway.

Research based on the released Ministry of Defence files identifies UA37 as traffic travelling roughly **40 miles east of Bentwaters** at **Flight Level 370**, placing it high above the region in established commercial airspace rather than low over the forest.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/rendlesham-forest-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[drdavidclarke.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">December 31, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2020</span></span></span>

This matters for two reasons.

First, the aircraft's position and altitude were entirely consistent with routine civil aviation. Nothing in the Watton entry suggests controllers regarded it as unusual.

Second, the reported visual observations from the forest involved lights apparently seen much closer to the ground, sometimes described as moving through trees or hovering near the base. A commercial aircraft cruising at approximately 37,000 feet on a recognised airway would not itself match those descriptions. The Watton log therefore records identified traffic while leaving the visual reports unresolved rather than linking the two.

## What the log does not record

The absence of certain information has become almost as important as the information that is present.

The surviving log contains **no record of an unidentified radar contact** associated with the reported lights. Later documentation reinforces that interpretation.

In a memorandum dated **26 February 1981**, Squadron Leader Derek Coumbe of RAF Watton stated that the controller on duty had been instructed to examine the radar display and that **"nothing was observed."** He added that these facts were recorded in the station logbook. Years later, Coumbe recalled that Bentwaters personnel repeatedly telephoned expecting radar confirmation, but despite maintaining watch, operators saw nothing unusual.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/rendlesham-forest-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[drdavidclarke.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">December 31, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2020</span></span></span>

Subsequent Ministry of Defence statements remained consistent with that position. Parliamentary answers have stated that the released files contain **no radar reports or radar tapes** confirming unidentified objects connected with Rendlesham, and later reviews similarly recorded no evidence of unusual radar returns in the surviving documentation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-questions/detail/2015-07-16/HL1550" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: questions-statements.parliament.uk">[UK Parliament]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">questions-statements.parliament.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UK Parliament Written questions and answers</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UK ParliamentWritten questions and answers - Written questions, answers and statements - UK Parliament...</span></span></span>

This does not prove that witnesses misidentified every light they reported. Radar has practical limitations, particularly for small, low-flying or weakly reflecting objects. However, the Watton log cannot reasonably be cited as evidence that radar independently tracked an unidentified craft over Rendlesham Forest. Instead, it documents a negative result: controllers checked for relevant traffic, identified one routine high-altitude aircraft, and recorded no [unexplained]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }}) target.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/rendlesham-forest-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[drdavidclarke.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">December 31, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2020</span></span></span>

## Why the Watton log remains important

The value of the Watton log lies less in what it appears to confirm than in what it securely establishes. Unlike recollections recorded years later, it is a contemporaneous operational record created during the incident itself.

Because the entry was written in real time, historians and investigators generally regard it as one of the strongest surviving pieces of documentary evidence relating to radar activity that night. It anchors several points with unusual certainty:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* Bentwaters did seek assistance from RAF radar controllers.
* Radar personnel checked for aircraft during the reported sightings.
* A known civil aircraft, UA37, was identified.
* No unidentified radar target was entered into the surviving log.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/rendlesham-forest-ufos/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[drdavidclarke.co.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest UFOs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">December 31, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 31, 2020</span></span></span>

</div>
