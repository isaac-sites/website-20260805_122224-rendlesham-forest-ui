## Introduction

The surviving audio recording made by Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }}) is one of the most important contemporaneous records from the Rendlesham Forest incident because it was created during the investigation itself rather than written from memory weeks later. As a result, it provides a crucial chronological check on Halt's later memorandum to the UK Ministry of Defence. Although the memorandum states that Halt entered the forest on **29 December 1980**, the tape, together with independent reconstructions of the timeline, shows that the recording belongs to the **night of 27–28 December 1980**. This one-day discrepancy is central to understanding the date errors in the official account because it demonstrates that the memo's chronology cannot be accepted without comparison against evidence created at the time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian Ridpath Appendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the Mo D</span></span></span>

## How the tape is dated to 27–28 December

Unlike the memorandum, the tape was not reconstructed from memory. Halt carried a handheld Lanier micro-cassette recorder during the field investigation and switched it on repeatedly to capture notable observations. The recording therefore preserves a sequence of events as they unfolded, albeit with interruptions caused by the recorder being stopped and restarted.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Transcript%20of%20Colonel%20Halt%20Tape%20%28Ian%20Ridpath%29.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avalon Library Transcri</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">pt of Col. Halt’s tape Transcribed bDecember 7, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2019</span></span></span>

Researchers have dated the recording to the early hours of **28 December 1980**, meaning it documents the overnight investigation that began on the evening of **27 December**. Ian Ridpath's detailed transcript explicitly identifies the tape as originating from "the early hours of 1980 December 28" and contrasts this with the first incident, which occurred in the early hours of 26 December.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Transcript%20of%20Colonel%20Halt%20Tape%20%28Ian%20Ridpath%29.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avalon Library Transcri</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">pt of Col. Halt’s tape Transcribed bDecember 7, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2019</span></span></span>

Independent chronologies compiled from [witness statements]({{ 'witness-dates/' | relative_url }}), [police records]({{ 'police-record/' | relative_url }}) and military documentation also place Halt's expedition on the night of **27–28 December**. These timelines consistently distinguish:

* First incident: night of **25–26 December** (early hours of 26 December).
* Subsequent light reports: night of **26–27 December**.
* Halt's recorded investigation: night of **27–28 December**.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://kb42.info/index.php/Rendlesham_Forest_Incident_%E2%80%94_Complete_Timeline" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: kb42.info">[KB42]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">kb42.info</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest Incident — Complete Timeline</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rendlesham Forest Incident — Complete Timeline - KB42...</span></span></span>

</div>

The agreement between these independent sources strengthens the conclusion that the tape belongs to 27–28 December rather than the date given in the memorandum.

## Recorded observations after midnight

The tape itself reflects an investigation already under way during the small hours of the morning rather than an operation beginning on the evening of 29 December. Halt and his team can be heard examining the reported landing site, discussing radiation measurements, observing flashing lights beyond the trees and later describing bright objects in the sky. Because these comments were dictated in real time, they provide a much firmer chronological anchor than a summary prepared over two weeks later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Transcript%20of%20Colonel%20Halt%20Tape%20%28Ian%20Ridpath%29.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avalon Library Transcri</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">pt of Col. Halt’s tape Transcribed bDecember 7, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2019</span></span></span>

The recording also demonstrates that the investigation was not a single uninterrupted event. Halt repeatedly stopped and restarted the recorder, producing an audio log of selected moments rather than a continuous soundtrack. Nevertheless, the sequence of observations corresponds closely with the accepted chronology of the night of 27–28 December.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Transcript%20of%20Colonel%20Halt%20Tape%20%28Ian%20Ridpath%29.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avalon Library Transcri</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">pt of Col. Halt’s tape Transcribed bDecember 7, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2019</span></span></span>

Importantly, nothing within the recording indicates that it belongs to a separate investigation on the following night. Instead, it fits naturally into the established sequence of reports occurring after renewed sightings prompted Halt to lead a party into Rendlesham Forest.

## Why 29 December is chronologically untenable

The memorandum dated 13 January 1981 states that Halt entered the forest on **29 December**, but this date conflicts with both contemporaneous and later evidence.

First, the tape itself documents the investigation that witnesses consistently identify as occurring after renewed reports on the night of 27–28 December. Since the recording was made during the operation, there is no credible basis for moving it forward by an entire day.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Transcript%20of%20Colonel%20Halt%20Tape%20%28Ian%20Ridpath%29.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avalon Library Transcri</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">pt of Col. Halt’s tape Transcribed bDecember 7, 2019...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 7, 2019</span></span></span>

## Why the tape matters for the chronology

The Halt tape occupies a unique position in the Rendlesham evidence because it is contemporaneous, independently preserved and directly connected to the investigation. It does not resolve the debate over what the witnesses observed, but it does resolve an important question of chronology.

For historians, investigators and sceptics alike, the tape demonstrates that the official memorandum's reference to **29 December** cannot be treated as the definitive date of Halt's forest excursion. Instead, the recording anchors the investigation to the **night of 27–28 December 1980**, making the memorandum's date another example of the one-day chronological errors that affect the official account.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Transcript%20of%20Colonel%20Halt%20Tape%20%28Ian%20Ridpath%29.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[avalonlibrary.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Avalon Library Transcri</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">pt of Col.     return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
