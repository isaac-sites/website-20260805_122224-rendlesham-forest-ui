## Introduction

The strongest independent evidence for correcting the date of the first Rendlesham Forest incident does not come from later witness recollections or retrospective analysis. It comes from Suffolk [police records]({{ 'police-record/' | relative_url }}) created as officers responded to calls from RAF Woodbridge. Those records place the first police attendance in the early hours of **26 December 1980 (Boxing Day)**, directly contradicting the **27 December** date later printed in Lieutenant Colonel [Charles Halt]({{ 'charles-halt/' | relative_url }})'s memorandum of 13 January 1981. Because the police logs were produced as part of routine operational activity, before the incident became internationally famous, they provide one of the most reliable chronological anchors available for reconstructing the opening phase of the case.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-rendlesham-forest-britains-roswell/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: Rendlesham Forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: Rendlesham Forest - Britain’s Roswell? - The SkepticApril 2, 2006...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 2, 2006</span></span></span>

## When Bentwaters contacted Suffolk police

The first unusual lights reported by United States Air Force security personnel were initially treated as a possible aircraft emergency rather than as a UFO event. Security police investigating lights beyond the eastern perimeter of RAF [Woodbridge]({{ 'woodbridge/' | relative_url }}) requested assistance, and the local Suffolk Constabulary was contacted shortly afterwards.

According to the surviving police records, officers were first dispatched at **4.11 am on 26 December 1980**. After attending the area, the officers reported that the only identifiable light they observed was [the beam]({{ 'the-beam/' | relative_url }}) from the Orford Ness [lighthouse]({{ 'lighthouse/' | relative_url }}) on the Suffolk coast. Whatever interpretation is placed on the servicemen's reports, the existence of this call fixes the military alarm to the early hours of Boxing Day rather than the following morning.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-rendlesham-forest-britains-roswell/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: Rendlesham Forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: Rendlesham Forest - Britain’s Roswell? - The SkepticApril 2, 2006...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 2, 2006</span></span></span>

The police were called a second time later that same morning. At approximately **10.30 am on 26 December**, officers returned to inspect the reported ground marks in the forest after daylight made examination possible. Police concluded that the small depressions were not persuasive evidence of a landed aircraft or unknown vehicle and regarded them as consistent with ordinary natural causes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-rendlesham-forest-britains-roswell/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[skeptic.org.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: Rendlesham Forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: Rendlesham Forest - Britain’s Roswell? - The SkepticApril 2, 2006...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 2, 2006</span></span></span>

## How the attendance record anchors 26 December

Unlike later memoirs or interviews, the police attendance record was generated during routine operational work. Its significance lies less in what officers thought they had seen than in the timing of their involvement.

Several features strengthen its value as chronological evidence:

* The police had no apparent reason to adjust the date after the event because they were responding to what was initially treated as a conventional emergency call.
* The first attendance and the daylight re-inspection both occurred on **26 December**, creating two independent time points within the same day.
* The timings correspond with the earliest [witness statements]({{ 'witness-dates/' | relative_url }}) from security personnel, which describe events during the night of **25–26 December**, rather than the night of 26–27 December.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham2c.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO - The witness statements 2...</span></span></span>

The chronology therefore rests on ordinary administrative records rather than on later reconstruction. Even researchers who disagree about what the witnesses observed generally accept that the police documentation places the first incident on 26 December.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-rendlesham-forest-britains-roswell/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[The Skeptic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: Rendlesham Forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: Rendlesham Forest - Britain’s Roswell? - The SkepticApril 2, 2006...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 2, 2006</span></span></span>

## Why the corrected date changes later comparisons

Correcting the first sighting from **27 December** to **26 December** is more than a matter of historical precision. The date determines which external records can legitimately be compared with the reported observations.

A one-day error affects:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* comparisons with police dispatch records;
* duty rosters for security personnel;
* astronomical events, including bright meteors recorded during that period;
* weather conditions;
* the spacing between the first security patrol incident and Charles Halt's later investigation.

</div>

Once the first episode is fixed to 26 December, the sequence of events becomes internally consistent. ## Why historians regard the police records as especially important

The Suffolk Constabulary records occupy a distinctive place in the Rendlesham chronology because they are independent of the United States Air Force chain of command and pre-date the public controversy that later surrounded the case. They neither confirm nor refute extraordinary interpretations of what witnesses believed they encountered. Instead, they establish something more fundamental: **when** official agencies became involved.

This distinction matters because debates over witness reliability, memory and later embellishment cannot alter the timestamp created by routine police dispatch. That is why researchers across differing interpretations—including sceptical investigators and writers otherwise sympathetic to the witnesses—have repeatedly relied on the police attendance record when correcting the official timeline. The evidence does not settle what happened in Rendlesham Forest, but it provides one of the clearest documentary reasons for dating the first incident to the early hours of **26 December 1980**, not 27 December.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2006/04/from-the-archive-rendlesham-forest-britains-roswell/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: skeptic.org.uk">[skeptic.org.uk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">skeptic.org.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Skeptic From the archive: Rendlesham Forest</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The SkepticFrom the archive: Rendlesham Forest - Britain’s Roswell? - The SkepticApril 2, 2006...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 2, 2006</span></span></span>
