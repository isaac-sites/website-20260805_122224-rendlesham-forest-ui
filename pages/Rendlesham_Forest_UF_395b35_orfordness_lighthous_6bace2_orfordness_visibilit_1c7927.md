## Introduction

Could witnesses in Rendlesham Forest actually have seen the Orfordness [Lighthouse]({{ 'lighthouse/' | relative_url }}) without seeing the lighthouse itself? The historical evidence indicates that they could. Before the lighthouse was decommissioned in 2013 and demolished in 2020, investigators repeatedly demonstrated that its powerful rotating light was visible from parts of the eastern forest while the tower remained concealed by trees, terrain and distance. This distinction is central to the Orfordness explanation. The question is not whether the lighthouse building was plainly visible, but whether its five-second flashes could appear as an isolated light beyond or between the trees. Contemporary [witness statements]({{ 'witness-dates/' | relative_url }}), later site investigations and photographic demonstrations all support the possibility that they could.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham1a.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original articleJanuary 5, 1985...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 5, 1985</span></span></span>

## The five-mile sightline from forest to coast

The Orfordness Lighthouse stood on the Suffolk coast, roughly five miles east-south-east of the areas where the principal observations occurred in Rendlesham Forest. Although that sounds distant, a powerful coastal navigation light was specifically designed to be seen over far greater ranges than this.

The geography is important. The eastern edge of Rendlesham Forest gradually falls away towards the coastal marshes and shingle of Orford Ness. Once observers reached suitable gaps near the forest edge, they were looking broadly along the same bearing as the lighthouse. Several early witness statements describe following a flashing light that never became significantly closer, behaviour consistent with a distant fixed source rather than an object moving through nearby woodland. Ed Cabansag described it as "a [beacon light]({{ 'beacon-light/' | relative_url }}) off in the distance", while John [Burroughs]({{ 'burroughs/' | relative_url }}) later identified it as a lighthouse.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendlesham_Forest_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rendlesham Forest incident</span></span></span>

The sightline also explains why witnesses could believe the light was inside the forest. At night there were few visual references for judging distance. Without seeing the coastline, the tower or the horizon, only the intermittent flash remained visible, making it easy for observers to underestimate how far away the source actually was.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham1a.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original articleJanuary 5, 1985...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 5, 1985</span></span></span>

## How trees and terrain hid the tower

The most common objection to the lighthouse explanation is that many visitors cannot simply stand anywhere in Rendlesham Forest and see the lighthouse. In reality, that observation supports rather than weakens the visibility argument.

The dense commercial pine plantation contained long rows of mature trunks, uneven clearings and varying ground levels. From many positions the lighthouse tower itself was completely hidden behind woodland or intervening terrain. Yet the lamp room sat high above the surrounding landscape, allowing the rotating beam to become visible through gaps in the trees or just above the distant tree line.

This created an unusual visual effect:

* the flash appeared without the tower being visible;
* the light emerged briefly between trunks as [the beam]({{ 'the-beam/' | relative_url }}) rotated;
* slight movements by observers caused the flash to seem to shift position because different gaps between trees opened and closed;
* with no visible coastline, the light appeared much nearer than its true location.

Ian Ridpath noted that the lighthouse could not be seen directly from the RAF [Woodbridge]({{ 'woodbridge/' | relative_url }}) East Gate, becoming visible only after moving further into the forest where the land falls away towards the coast. His photographs document exactly this change in visibility.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/photos.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO photo album</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO photo albumMarch 1, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 1, 2021</span></span></span>

This distinction is often overlooked in later discussions. Seeing the rotating flash did not require an unobstructed view of the lighthouse structure itself.

## Photographic demonstrations before decommissioning

One of the strongest pieces of evidence comes from photographs taken while the lighthouse was still operating.

In November 1983, Ian Ridpath visited the alleged landing area with local forester Vince Thurkettle. During the night they photographed the active lighthouse from within the forest. The images show the bright flashing light appearing between surviving trees at the eastern forest edge while the lighthouse building itself remains invisible. According to Ridpath's account, the beam appeared to hover only a few feet above the ground because the forest stands slightly higher than the coastal land beyond. As the observers moved, the apparent position of the light also shifted, closely matching descriptions from the original witnesses.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham1a.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original articleJanuary 5, 1985...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 5, 1985</span></span></span>

The BBC also filmed the location during the same investigation. Ridpath reported that even a previously sceptical television cameraman found the lighthouse flash unexpectedly conspicuous once standing in the forest at night. Frames from the broadcast show the flash visible over Vince Thurkettle's shoulder despite the surrounding trees obscuring the lighthouse itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham1a.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ian Ridpath Rendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original article</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathRendlesham Forest UFO explained – the original articleJanuary 5, 1985...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 5, 1985</span></span></span>
